# Example duets

All are arrangements of the built-in demo songs (original tunes, not
covers), produced with `duet-arranger demo pop|ballad|waltz|sixeight -o examples`.

In every one, Guitar I is a complete fingerstyle part — melody on top, its
own bass on the strong beats and chord changes, harmony in between — and
Guitar II adds the groove and more harmony: never the very same note as
Guitar I at once, but chord tones in other octaves, the bass an octave lower
on chord changes and, in the choruses, the tune itself in octaves.

* `demo-pop.*` — drum-driven version: Guitar I plays the melody over a
  bass on beats 1 and 3; Guitar II plays the bass groove in between, the
  piano's syncopated chord rhythm and kick/snare percussion.
* `demo-ballad.*` — no drums: Guitar I has bass and chord tones on the beats
  under the tune; Guitar II adds flowing arpeggios, open-string resonance and,
  where the tune sits low, a soft harmonic descant above it. No percussion.
* `demo-waltz.*` — a 3/4 ballad in Bb: Guitar I strikes the piano intro's
  chords on every beat, Guitar II rings bass, open strings and harmonics under
  them; then Guitar I plays melody, bass and harmony and Guitar II an
  eighth-note arpeggio.
* `demo-sixeight.*` — a 6/8 ballad in Bb (dotted quarter = 50): chords on
  each dotted quarter in the intro, then Guitar I plays the melody with its
  bass on beats 1 and 4 and chord tones rolling on the eighths while the tune
  holds, the texture of a solo 6/8 fingerstyle piece; Guitar II's arpeggio
  sits between Guitar I's inner voice and the tune.

Open a `.atelier.json` in Atelier with **File → Import Atelier project**, or
read the `.tab.txt` in any text editor.
