# Example duets

Both are arrangements of the built-in demo song (an original tune, not a
cover), produced with `duet-arranger demo pop|ballad|waltz -o examples`.

* `demo-pop.*` — drum-driven version: Guitar II plays bass, the piano's
  syncopated chord rhythm and kick/snare percussion.
* `demo-ballad.*` — no drums: flowing arpeggios, open-string resonance and
  harmonics, no percussion.

Open a `.atelier.json` in Atelier with **File → Import Atelier project**, or
read the `.tab.txt` in any text editor.
* `demo-waltz.*` — a 3/4 ballad in Bb: Guitar I strikes the piano intro's
  chords on every beat, Guitar II rings bass, open strings and harmonics under
  them, then an eighth-note arpeggio under the sung melody.
* `demo-sixeight.*` — a 6/8 ballad in Bb (dotted quarter = 50): chords on
  each dotted quarter in the intro, then an eighth-note arpeggio under the
  melody, the texture of many triple-time pop ballads.
